## Differences that remain

- `workspace-recording-*` (4 pairs, 0.06–0.12% of pixels): the diff is confined to the
  200×80 `<canvas>` where the live frequency bars are drawn (bounding box exactly 200 px wide,
  for example x 620–820 at 1440 px). The bars are drawn from Chromium's fake microphone at the
  moment of capture, so two recordings never produce the same frame. Everything else in those
  screenshots (panel, pinging dot, label, layout) is identical.

All other 64 pairs are pixel-identical (0 px), in light and dark, at 1440×900 and 390×844.
Both apps were fed identical fixture data; see `scripts/compare_screenshots.py`.
